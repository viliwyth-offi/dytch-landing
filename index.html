Why direct freight

Keep the margin on your side

Brokers take a cut of every load, and load boards turn your lanes into a race to the bottom. Carriers who work direct shippers routinely report 20–40% better rates per mile than the boards pay, on freight that repeats.

  • You talk rates with the shipper, instead of taking what is left after a middleman.
  • A shipper who likes your service comes back, and that freight repeats without a broker.
  • Shippers invite 3 to 6 carriers into their annual bids. The job is being one of the names they already know.
  • Less time refreshing load boards, more time running loads.

Straight answers

Questions carriers actually ask

What does it cost?

Priced for small fleets, month to month, no 12-month contract. Agencies in this space charge $1,000 a month and up; we start well under that because one person runs the work. Email your lanes and you get a straight number, not a sales sequence.

Do you cold-call shippers?

No. Everything runs by email, written to the person who books the freight, with your lanes, equipment and authority up front. If a shipper wants a call, they will ask you directly.

How fast do leads come?

Outreach starts within days of your first email. Replies depend on your lanes and freight type; the free 3-target sample shows you the quality before you spend a dollar.

Why no case studies or logos?

dytch is a new, one-person agency and we won't borrow credibility. What you get is hands-on work from the same person you email, and a free sample of it before you commit to anything.

What do you need from me?

Your lanes, equipment, and what good freight looks like for you. For driver recruiting: pay, home time and requirements. That is the whole onboarding.

Load boards already work for me. Why change?

Keep the board for gaps. We build the thing boards never create: shippers who call you first, at rates the board can't post.
